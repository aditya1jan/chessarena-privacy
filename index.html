Privacy Policy

Chess Arena
Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aditya ("we", "us") handles information in the Chess Arena Android app. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game settings and win/loss/draw record are stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information (see section 3).

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone so it can remember your preferences:

  • Your win, loss and draw record.
  • Settings such as the selected board theme and the sound on/off choice.

This information is not sent to us or to any server we operate. It is deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Chess games are played and calculated on your device; the computer opponents do not require an internet connection to play.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and Google may automatically collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier for your device)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and operating system information, app identifier, and diagnostic data
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads shown and clicked

Google acts as an independent controller of this data for advertising purposes. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less personalized.

4. Permissions the App uses

PermissionWhy

The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools in the App.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the data we store is kept on your device, you control it. To delete it, clear the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the App. For data held by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.

8. Security

We keep data on your device in the App's private storage. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we do not transmit your game data off your device.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Since we do not hold your personal information on our servers, most requests can be handled on your device or through Google's controls described above. You can also contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do, and significant changes will be reflected in the App's store listing or in an app update.
